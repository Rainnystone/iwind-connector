# Analytics routing

Analytics is the last native branch, not a fallback. First confirm that no predefined stock, fund, index, economic, or document tool can directly express the requested result.

| Tool | Use and material choices | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `get_financial_data` | A supported custom result such as cross-entity aggregation, weighting, ranking, or a composite measure; state the entity universe, exact dates/report period, input metrics, formula, weighting, and output unit. | Free-float market cap from free-float shares and same-date price is transparent arithmetic handled by stock tools. Do not use for entity discovery, a predefined single-entity property, a predefined index aggregate, an economic series, or transparent presentation arithmetic on already returned values. |

Unsupported assets, actions, markets, and services remain out of scope. Analytics cannot manufacture their data or substitute another source.
